Privacy Policy

Verdura

Last updated: 9 October 2026

The short version: Verdura has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no servers of its own. Your plants, care logs, photos and settings stay on your device, and the app sends nothing to us. The only thing it fetches from the internet is its typeface, from Google Fonts. The older iPhone version on the App Store today may also contain Google's advertising SDK; see section 4.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any information through Verdura. There is no sign-up, login or account of any kind, and the app does not send your plants, care logs, photos, settings or any device identifier to us or to any analytics, advertising or tracking service. Verdura does not use your location: you choose your hemisphere for seasonal care yourself.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Everything you enter is stored only in Verdura's own storage on your device. This includes:

  • Plants: name, species, photo, watering dates, light, humidity, pot size, soil and your schedule settings
  • Care logs: what you did (watering, fertilizing, misting, repotting, pruning, pest treatment or a note), when you did it and any note you add
  • Photos you take or choose for your plants
  • Settings: language, theme, units, hemisphere, reminder time and style, snoozed reminders, default light and humidity, and how your plant list is shown

Verdura also keeps its built-in plant species database and the downloaded typeface on your device. We cannot see any of this data. The app does not add encryption of its own; your data is protected by your device's security, such as its passcode. If you use device backups (iCloud or computer backups on iPhone, Google backup on Android), your phone may include Verdura's data in them. Apple and Google handle those backups under their own privacy policies.

3. Third-Party Services

The current version of Verdura contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, attribution or tracking SDKs. Two outside services are involved:

  • Google Fonts — Verdura shows text in the Inter typeface, which it downloads from Google Fonts (fonts.gstatic.com) the first time it needs it and then keeps on your device. Like any internet request, this shows Google your device's IP address. The request contains none of your plant data. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — sell the app and process your payment (see section 9).

4. The iPhone Version on the App Store Today

This policy describes the current version of Verdura, which is the one on Google Play. The iPhone version on the App Store, 1.2.52, was built in April 2026, before we removed Google's Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) from the app's code, and may still contain it. Verdura never had real ad units, so that version shows no ads. If the SDK is included, it may start when the app opens and send Google technical information about your device, such as its IP address, under Google's privacy policy. We do not receive any of it. The current code no longer contains this SDK, so future iPhone versions will not include it.

5. Permissions

  • Camera — only when you choose to take a photo of a plant.
  • Photos — to let you choose a photo of a plant with your phone's photo picker. Verdura only uses the photos you pick.
  • Notifications — to send care reminders (see section 6).
  • On Android, Verdura can also schedule reminders at exact times, restore them after your phone restarts, and use the internet to download its typeface.

Verdura does not access your location, contacts or microphone.

6. Reminders and Notifications

If you turn on reminders, Verdura schedules local notifications on your device. They are created on the device itself: there is no notification server and no push token. A reminder can show a plant's name on your lock screen. You can turn reminders off in Verdura's settings or in your phone's settings.

7. Export, Import and Feedback

  • Export as JSON or CSV creates a file on your device and opens your phone's share sheet. You decide where the file goes.
  • Import from JSON reads only the backup file you pick.
  • Send feedback opens your email app with a message to heldig.lab@pm.me. We receive only what you choose to send.

8. Deleting Your Data

Deleting a plant removes it and its care history. Settings → Reset all data deletes all plants and care logs. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device, apart from any copies in your device backups.

9. Purchases

Verdura is a paid app with no in-app purchases, no subscriptions and no ads. The purchase is processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive, store or have access to any payment information; the store's own privacy policy governs that transaction.

10. Children's Privacy

Verdura is not directed at children under 13. Because the app sends us no information, we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

11. Changes to This Policy

If we change this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
