Privacy Policy

TinniCalm

Last updated: 3 October 2026

The short version: TinniCalm has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your sounds, presets, settings and listening history stay on your device. On Android we use advertising-measurement SDKs from Meta and TikTok to learn which ads lead to installs and purchases; they receive your device's advertising ID and app events, never your health information.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, location, health or medical information, audio, or anything you set up in the app. The only data that leaves your device is what the services in section 3 need to work:

  • Purchase status (whether you have unlocked premium), handled by RevenueCat and Google Play or the App Store
  • On Android: your device's advertising ID and app events (install, app open, purchase and its price), shared with Meta and TikTok to measure our ads

2. Data Storage

All app data — including your sound preferences, custom presets, and settings — is stored exclusively on your device using local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Google Play Billing / Apple App Store — process payments. We never see your payment details.
  • RevenueCat — keeps track of whether your purchase is active, using an anonymous app user ID.
  • Meta (Facebook) SDK, Android only — install and purchase events with the advertising ID, to measure and optimise our ads on Facebook and Instagram. Meta Privacy Policy.
  • TikTok Business SDK, Android only — install and purchase events with the advertising ID, to measure our TikTok ads. TikTok Privacy Policy.
  • Apple SKAdNetwork, iOS only — anonymous, aggregated install attribution with no personal data.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads), which stops these services from linking events to you.

4. Accounts & Authentication

TinniCalm does not require or offer user accounts. There is no sign-up, login, or authentication of any kind.

5. In-App Purchases

TinniCalm offers an optional one-time premium unlock. The transaction is processed entirely by Google Play or the App Store. We do not receive, store, or have access to any payment information; the store's own privacy policy governs that transaction.

6. Children's Privacy

TinniCalm is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. International Users

The services in section 3 may process data outside your country under their own safeguards. You can ask us about your data or request deletion at contact@heldiglab.com.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we change this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

9. Contact Us

10. Health Disclaimer

TinniCalm is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. The sounds and features provided are for relaxation and general wellness purposes only. If you experience tinnitus or any hearing-related condition, please consult a qualified healthcare professional.
